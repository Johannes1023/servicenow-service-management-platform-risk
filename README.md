# 🔄 ServiceNow Service Management & Platform Risk

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focused on the use of ServiceNow as a central service management platform across multiple business and technology units.

The platform supported incident reporting, ticket management, escalation and coordination between business users, IT operations and technical support teams.

I worked with service management, technology, application and risk stakeholders to understand how critical operational processes depended on ServiceNow and where platform, process or configuration risks could affect day-to-day business operations.

My role combined project management, service management expertise and technology risk assessment with active involvement in discussions around incident workflows, prioritization, platform dependencies and operational controls.

---

## 🎯 Business Challenge

Different business units relied on ServiceNow to report incidents, request support and coordinate the resolution of technology issues.

As usage increased, the platform became an important part of the operating model itself.

The challenge was therefore not only to assess whether incidents were being documented correctly, but whether the overall ServiceNow setup supported fast and reliable incident handling across the organization.

Key questions included:

- Are critical incidents identified and prioritized correctly?
- Are tickets routed to the right teams without unnecessary delay?
- Are escalation paths clear and consistently applied?
- Are service and application dependencies sufficiently transparent?
- What happens if ServiceNow itself or one of its critical integrations becomes unavailable?
- Are roles, access rights and workflow configurations appropriately controlled?

---

## 🧩 My Role

I worked across service management, application, infrastructure and business teams to understand how ServiceNow was used in practice and where operational or technology risks could emerge.

I was involved in discussions around incident handling, workflow design, service dependencies, access management and platform configuration.

Where deeper technical knowledge was required, I worked with ServiceNow specialists, application owners and IT operations SMEs to understand the platform setup, challenge assumptions and agree on practical improvements.

My role was to connect the operational process with the underlying technology and make sure that risks were understood from an end-to-end service perspective.

## 🚨 Incident Management

A major part of the project focused on how incidents moved through the ServiceNow process.

```text
Business / User
      │
      ▼
Incident Created
      │
      ▼
Classification & Priority
      │
      ▼
Assignment Group
      │
      ▼
Technical Investigation
      │
      ▼
Escalation if Required
      │
      ▼
Resolution
      │
      ▼
Closure & Documentation
```

The key concern was whether this process worked reliably for both routine incidents and high-impact events.

Together with the relevant teams, we looked at areas such as:

- Incident categorization and priority
- Assignment to responsible support teams
- Escalation rules
- SLA and response expectations
- Communication during major incidents

The objective was to reduce situations where technically critical issues were delayed because of incorrect categorization, routing or ownership.

---

## 🔴 Major Incident Management

High-impact incidents required a different level of coordination.

We reviewed how major incidents were identified, escalated and communicated across technical and business teams.

Key topics included:

- Criteria for declaring a major incident
- Clear ownership during critical events
- Escalation to specialist and management teams
- Communication with affected business units
- Documentation of decisions and recovery actions

The focus was on ensuring that severe incidents could move quickly from ticket creation to coordinated response.

---

## 🧩 Service Dependencies & CMDB

Incident management becomes more effective when teams understand which business services and applications depend on the affected technology.

We therefore also considered the quality and usability of service dependency information.

```text
Business Service
      │
      ▼
Application
      │
      ▼
Technology Component
      │
      ▼
Incident
      │
      ▼
Business Impact
```

Where CMDB and service information were available, they could help support impact analysis, routing and prioritization.

The challenge was to make sure that dependency information remained useful and sufficiently accurate for operational decision-making.

---

## 🔐 Access & Platform Control

Because ServiceNow contained operational information and controlled important workflows, access management was another relevant area.

The discussions included:

- User and support roles
- Privileged administrator access
- Separation of responsibilities
- Access to sensitive incident information
- Changes to workflows and platform configuration

The goal was to reduce the risk that inappropriate access or configuration changes could affect important service management processes.

---

## 🔗 Integrations & Platform Dependencies

ServiceNow did not operate in isolation.

The platform depended on integrations with other enterprise systems and technical tools.

Examples included:

- Monitoring and alerting systems
- Identity and access management
- Application and infrastructure tools
- Notification services
- Configuration and asset information

We therefore also looked at the operational impact if integrations failed or information was not transferred correctly.

This was particularly relevant where automated workflows depended on data from other systems.

---

## ⚠️ ServiceNow Platform Risk

An important part of the project was treating ServiceNow itself as a technology dependency.

Typical risk scenarios included:

- Platform outages
- Incorrect ticket routing
- Misconfigured workflows
- Failed integrations
- Incorrect priority classification
- Incomplete or outdated service information
- Excessive administrative privileges

The objective was to understand which weaknesses could delay incident response or reduce transparency during operational disruptions.

---

## 📈 Results

- Improved transparency of ServiceNow dependencies and operational risks
- Strengthened incident prioritization, routing and escalation processes
- Improved understanding of service and application dependencies during incidents
- Clarified ownership and access responsibilities across ServiceNow processes
- Created clearer priorities for platform, workflow and process improvements
